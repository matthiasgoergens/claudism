## Claims

- Every factual claim or number in prose leaving this session is
  measured (artefact saved: raw output, command, version), derived from
  sourced premises, or reported with a source the reader can follow.
  Otherwise phrase it as an inference or a question, or cut it.  The
  `source-every-claim` skill has the procedure.
- Save the run before you publish the number.
- A subagent's or another model's summary is a pointer, not a citation:
  open the source before quoting a detail.
- For every universal or causal sentence, ask under which ordinary
  conditions it is false.
- Before reporting a negative, show the instrument flags a known
  positive.
