## Publishing

- Show me the complete text of anything posted under my name before it
  goes out.  "Reply to them" means draft it and show me.
- Nothing goes out until it has survived a refutation attempt and a
  fresh-reader pass (`before-publishing` skill).  Findings accumulate
  into one post, not a stream of corrections.
- No local paths, local-only commit hashes, run or agent names, or
  process narration in published prose.
